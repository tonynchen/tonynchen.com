隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 28 日 · English

心声是一款 iPad 上的辅助沟通（AAC）App，给不能说话的普通话使用者用。这份政策说明 App 会用到哪些数据、存在哪里、谁能看到。

默认：数据只在这台 iPad 上

心声不联网也能用，不需要注册账号，没有广告，也没有任何第三方 SDK 或追踪工具。

默认情况下，您的常用语、设置、联系人、说过的话和使用记录都只保存在这台 iPad 上。如果这台 iPad 开了 iCloud 云备份，这些数据也会随 iPad 一起备份到您自己的 iCloud。

心声读不到整个通讯录。家人在“设置 › 联系人”里用 Apple 的联系人选择器选人时，只会把选中的那张名片的名字、选定的一个电话号码或邮箱，以及名片上的照片存进心声。

加入家人的 iCloud 共享以后

家人可以在自己的设备上用心声向这台 iPad 发出 iCloud 共享邀请。只有在您接受邀请以后，App 才会把下面这些数据同步到那位家人的 iCloud（通过 Apple 的 CloudKit）：

  • 常用语、设置和联系人（按钮上的称呼、名片上的名字、FaceTime 和 iMessage 用的电话号码或邮箱，以及名片照片）。
  • 说出的句子。这些句子可能包含健康信息，比如哪里疼、有什么症状。私密话题除外：说了什么、选了哪个按钮都不会同步，使用记录里只写“私密”（在“设置 › 常用语”里可以把话题设成私密）。
  • 使用记录，比如选了哪些按钮、哪些点按被忽略或点在了空白处、“叫人”的开始和停止、打开和离开 App、打开设置、存常用语、进入休息屏幕、给谁打 FaceTime 或发消息（不记录消息内容）、同步状态，以及出错的情况。
  • 设备状态：App 和 iPadOS 的版本、设备型号、电量和充电状态、声音从哪里播放、音量、用的是哪个语音、设备温度状态、辅助触控是否打开、当前显示的页面、App 启动和意外退出的次数、这台 iPad 上存了多少句话和使用记录有多大，以及 App 生成的一个随机设备编号（用来区分不同的 iPad）。

谁能看到

这些数据保存在那位家人的 iCloud 里。那位家人可以在自己设备上的心声里看到这些数据，也可以修改您的常用语、设置和联系人，还可以邀请其他人加入这个共享。

心声的开发者收不到其他家庭的数据。开发者只能看到自己的家人共享给开发者的数据，并且只用来帮助自己的家人和改进心声。心声不会出售或分享任何数据。

数据保存在哪里

同步的数据由 Apple 保存在那位家人的 iCloud 里，适用 Apple 隐私政策。心声没有自己的服务器。

另外，如果在 iPad 的“设置 › 隐私与安全性 › 分析与改进”里打开了“与 App 开发者共享”，Apple 会把心声的崩溃报告等诊断信息转给开发者。您可以随时在那里关掉。

打电话和发消息

在心声里打 FaceTime 或发 iMessage 时，App 会交给 Apple 的 FaceTime 或“信息”去完成；分享到微信时，由您在系统的分享菜单里选择微信。之后的内容由这些 App 按各自的隐私政策处理。

停止同步和删除数据

  • 要停止同步：在“设置 › 关于”里选“停止和家人同步”（在顶部消息框上按住 1.5 秒可以打开设置），或者删除 App。删除以后，如果在同一个 Apple 账户下重新安装，同步会自动恢复，除非您已经停止了和家人同步。
  • 已经同步的数据会一直保存在那位家人的 iCloud 里，直到那位家人把它删除。要删除这些数据，请联系那位家人。需要帮助的话，可以发邮件到 aac@tonynchen.com。
  • 删除 App 会删掉这台 iPad 上心声的所有数据（已有的 iCloud 云备份里可能还留着一份）。

政策变更

这份政策如果有变化，会在这个页面上更新，并修改上面的更新日期。

联系方式

有任何问题，请发邮件到 aac@tonynchen.com。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026 · 中文

心声 (Xīnshēng) is an augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) app for iPad, for Mandarin speakers who can’t speak. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it’s kept, and who can see it.

By default, your data stays on this iPad

心声 works offline. It needs no account, shows no ads, and contains no third-party SDKs or trackers.

By default, your phrases, settings, contacts, what you’ve said and your usage history are stored only on this iPad. If the iPad uses iCloud Backup, they’re also included in the iPad’s backup in your own iCloud.

心声 can’t read your address book. When a family member picks someone in 设置 › 联系人 with Apple’s contact picker, only that card’s name, the one phone number or email they choose, and the card’s photo are copied into 心声.

If you join a family member’s iCloud share

A family member can use 心声 on their own device to send this iPad an iCloud sharing invitation. Only after you accept it does the app sync the following to that family member’s iCloud (through Apple’s CloudKit):

  • Phrases, settings and contacts (the name on each button, the name on the address-book card, the phone number or email used for FaceTime and iMessage, and the card’s photo).
  • Sentences you speak. These may include health information, such as where it hurts or other symptoms. Topics marked private (in 设置 › 常用语) are the exception: neither the words nor which button was chosen is synced, and the usage log only says “private”.
  • Usage events, such as which buttons you select, taps that were ignored or landed on empty space, 叫人 (“call someone”) alarms starting and stopping, opening and leaving the app, opening settings, saving phrases, the rest screen, whom you call on FaceTime or message (not the message itself), sync status, and errors.
  • Device status: app and iPadOS version, device model, battery level and charging, where sound is playing, the volume, which voice is in use, the device’s temperature state, whether AssistiveTouch is on, which screen is showing, how often the app has started or quit unexpectedly, how many sentences are stored on the iPad and the size of the usage log, and a random ID the app creates to tell your iPads apart.

Who can see it

This data is stored in that family member’s iCloud. They can see it in 心声 on their own device, change your phrases, settings and contacts, and invite other people to the share.

The developer of 心声 doesn’t receive other families’ data. The developer sees only what their own family shares with them, and uses it only to help their family and to improve 心声. 心声 never sells or shares data.

Where it’s stored

Apple stores synced data in the family member’s iCloud under the Apple Privacy Policy. 心声 has no servers of its own.

Separately, if Share with App Developers is on in the iPad’s Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements, Apple passes 心声’s crash reports and similar diagnostics to the developer. You can turn this off there at any time.

Calls and messages

When you start a FaceTime call or send an iMessage from 心声, the app hands it to Apple’s FaceTime or Messages. To share to WeChat, you choose WeChat in the system share sheet. From there, those apps handle it under their own privacy policies.

Stop syncing and delete your data

  • To stop syncing, choose 停止和家人同步 (“stop syncing with family”) in 设置 › 关于 (to open 设置, press and hold the message box at the top for 1.5 seconds), or delete the app. If you delete the app and later reinstall it on the same Apple Account, syncing resumes unless you’ve already stopped syncing with family.
  • Data that has already synced stays in the family member’s iCloud until they delete it. To have it deleted, ask that family member. If you need help, email aac@tonynchen.com.
  • Deleting the app removes all of 心声’s data from this iPad (an existing iCloud Backup may still hold a copy).
