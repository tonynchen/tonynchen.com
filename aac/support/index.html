English

心声是一款 iPad 上的辅助沟通（AAC）App，给不能说话的普通话使用者用。用眼控设备（通过 iPadOS 的辅助触控和停留控制）或者用手指选按钮，App 就会把话读出来。不联网也能用，不需要注册账号，没有广告。

联系我们

有问题或建议，请发邮件到 aac@tonynchen.com。

帮助

回到心声

离开了心声（比如打完 FaceTime，或者回到了主屏幕）：打开辅助触控菜单，选“回到说话”。

停止“叫人”

“叫人”会发出提示音，而且越来越响。要停下来：在屏幕上按住“按住停止”1 秒。只点一下不会停。

Support

中文

心声 (Xīnshēng) is an augmentative and alternative communication (AAC) app for iPad, for Mandarin speakers who can’t speak. Select buttons with an eye tracker (through iPadOS AssistiveTouch and Dwell Control) or by touch, and the app speaks for you. It works offline, with no account and no ads.

Contact

For questions or feedback, email aac@tonynchen.com.

Help

Get back to 心声

If you’ve left the app, for example after a FaceTime call or on the Home Screen, open the AssistiveTouch menu and choose 回到说话 (“back to talking”).

Stop 叫人

叫人 (“call someone”) plays an alert that keeps getting louder. To stop it, press and hold 按住停止 (“hold to stop”) on the screen for one second. A single tap won’t stop it.
